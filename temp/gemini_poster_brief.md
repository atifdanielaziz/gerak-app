# Gerak Marketing Poster Brief — for AI Image Generation

This document is self-contained. Everything needed to generate the posters is below — brand identity, exact wording, and critical accuracy rules. Do not invent or assume any product detail not stated here.

---

## 1. What Gerak is

Gerak is a campus super-app for Malaysian university students and staff, offering three services:

- **Gerak Car** — campus ride-hailing (point-to-point rides within/around campus)
- **Gerak Jubah** — graduation robe ("jubah") pickup/delivery for convocation
- **Gerak Rental** — peer-to-peer car rental by the hour or by the day

Gerak is a **PWA (Progressive Web App)** — installed by visiting the website and "Add to Home Screen." **There is no native app on the Apple App Store or Google Play.** Never include App Store / Google Play badges, download buttons, or QR codes implying a native app download. If a QR code or link is shown, it should point to the website: **gerakmy.com**.

Gerak currently operates across **13 Malaysian universities**: UMPSA, UiTM, UMK, UKM, UIAM, UUM, UniSZA, UTP, UPM, UM, UPSI, UMS, and UNIMAS. Do not depict or reference only one university (e.g. "UMPSA-only") unless a specific poster is explicitly for a UMPSA-only campaign — none of the posters below are.

## 2. Brand kit

**Logo / wordmark:** The word "gerak" in lowercase, bold sans-serif. The letters "g-e-r" are dark navy, the letters "a-k" are red. Paired with a small icon mark: a rounded-square badge (navy or red background) containing a simple white arrow/chevron pointing right, OR the official icon — an infinity-loop symbol formed by a navy circle (left) and a red loop (right), resembling a stylized "∞". Either logo treatment is acceptable; keep it small and in a corner (top-left is standard), never as the dominant visual element.

**Colors:**
- Primary red: `#EF4444` (hex) — the main brand accent, used for CTAs, highlights, the "ak" in the wordmark
- Red hover/darker: `#DC2626`
- Dark navy/near-black: `#0B1220` or `#0F172A` — used for backgrounds, text, or as the contrasting dark tone
- White: `#FFFFFF` — primary background or text-on-dark
- Muted gray-blue for secondary text on dark backgrounds: `#A7B0C4`

Use ONLY these colors (plus black/white/grayscale as needed). Do not introduce unrelated colors like green, purple, or gold. A poster should be either dark-navy-dominant with red accents, or red-dominant with navy accents/text — pick one per poster, don't mix evenly.

**Typography:** Clean, bold, modern sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Helvetica, or similar geometric sans). Headlines are bold/heavy weight. Avoid script, serif, or decorative fonts — Gerak's visual identity is clean and functional, not ornate.

**Visual style:** Flat design, bold color blocks, clean vector shapes, high contrast, generous whitespace. Avoid busy photo-realistic scenes, stock-photo aesthetics, gradients-as-decoration, or cluttered backgrounds. Simple vector illustrations (a car silhouette, a route/pin icon, a clock icon) are good; avoid literal photographs of people or cars unless specifically requested.

**Icons:** Simple line-style or filled vector icons only — no emoji, no clip-art style icons.

---

## 3. Critical accuracy rules — do not violate these

These are real product facts. Getting them wrong on a public poster is a real problem, not a style choice:

1. **No App Store / Google Play badges or native-app download language anywhere.** Gerak is PWA-only.
2. **Gerak Rental only has two booking types: "Hourly" and "Full-day."** There is no "Weekly" option. Do not show a 3-way Hourly/Daily/Weekly selector.
3. **Gerak Rental's minimum booking is 1 hour**, not 30 minutes. Never say "as low as half an hour."
4. **Do not show a mock booking UI with a separate "Return Date" field for hourly bookings** — hourly bookings just have a start time + duration. (A "Return"/"end date" concept only applies to full-day, multi-day bookings.)
5. **Never show two dates a year apart** (e.g. pickup 1/3/2021, return 1/3/2022) — bookings are hours to a few days, not months or years.
6. **Do not name one specific university as if it's the only one Gerak serves** (e.g. don't say "only for UMPSA students" or show only UMPSA's campus/venue names) — Gerak serves 13 universities.
7. **If showing a mock phone/app screen, keep it simple and generic** — a route line on a map, a fare amount, a "Confirm Booking" button. Don't invent specific driver names, exact prices, or UI copy not given to you.
8. **No fake iOS/Android status bar** (the "9:41, battery, wifi" strip) drawn inside a phone mockup — if showing a phone frame, leave that space alone or keep it minimal.

## 7. General instructions for whoever runs this brief

- Generate each option as its own separate image — 9 posters total (3 per service).
- Keep all text in Bahasa Malaysia exactly as written above (do not translate or rephrase it) — except CTA button text that's already in English ("Book Slot Anda" mixes languages deliberately, keep as-is).
- Match the brand colors and logo treatment in Section 2 on every poster.
- If a mock app/phone screen is included, follow every rule in Section 3 — these are the mistakes to specifically avoid.
- After generating, do a final check: does the poster show anything not described in this brief (an invented feature, a wrong date, a native-app badge, a single-university claim)? If yes, remove or correct it before finalizing.
